About

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Kigali (U.o.K), currently in my second year, building a foundation in programming, problem-solving, and software development. Alongside my studies, I work as a freelance full-stack web developer, building and maintaining production platforms for real clients — from a student learning resource site to an online TV-subscription marketplace.

What sets my work apart is the combination of two skill sets that don't usually sit in the same person: I build web applications end-to-end, and I also work hands-on with networking infrastructure — routing, VLANs, and DHCP configuration — giving me a systems-level understanding of how the applications I build actually reach people.

I also lead. I've served as an active leader of science clubs through school, and in 2026 I took 1st place at a Google-organized hackathon, contributing problem-solving and team leadership to a winning build under time pressure.
